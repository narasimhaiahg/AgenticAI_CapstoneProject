# Merchant Dispute Handling Procedure (synthetic training document)

## 2.1 Goods or services not received
When the customer recognises the merchant but reports that goods or services were not received or not fulfilled,
classify the case as a merchant dispute and route it to Dispute Operations. Record the expected delivery date and any
merchant contact attempts reported by the customer. Fraud investigation is not required unless independent anomalies exist.

## 2.2 Cancellation and refund not received
When the customer recognises the merchant and reports a cancelled order or subscription that was not refunded,
classify as a merchant dispute and route to Dispute Operations. Record the cancellation date and reference if provided.

## 2.3 Incorrect amount
When the customer recognises the merchant but the charged amount differs from the agreed amount, classify as a
merchant dispute (amount) and route to Dispute Operations with the agreed and charged amounts recorded.
